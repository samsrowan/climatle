    What is Climatle?

    Climatle is a daily guessing game designed to help people learn about climate change mitigation across countries. Each day, you're shown three visualizations of a mystery country's climate profile — its sectoral greenhouse gas emissions, its electricity generation mix, and its emissions trajectory over time — and you have six attempts to guess which country it is.

    After each guess, you receive feedback on how similar your guess is to the target country: three cosine-similarity scores (sectoral GHG, electricity mix, and GHG trajectory), geographic distance and direction, and a GHG-per-capita comparison. Use the clues to narrow down your next guess.

    Hard mode

    Toggle Hard mode above the guess input (before your first guess only) to play without the geographic hints — you'll see the three similarity scores and the per-capita comparison, but no distance or direction. Results shared from hard mode are marked with diamond markers; normal mode uses circles.

    Similarity scores

    • Sector sim. — cosine similarity of the two countries' sectoral GHG share vectors (24 subsectors; international aviation and shipping are excluded since they're not cleanly attributable to a single country).
    • Elec. sim. — cosine similarity of the electricity-mix shares (9 sources).
    • GHG sim. — shape of the GHG trajectory 1990–2024. Computed as a Pearson correlation of each country's index-normalized series, rescaled so 100% means identical growth pattern, 50% means uncorrelated, and 0% means opposite (one grew while the other declined).

    Data sources

    • Sectoral GHG emissions (2021): Minx et al., 2021 (Earth System Science Data), GWP100 AR6 weighting. Covers CO2, CH4, N2O, and fluorinated gases across 24 subsectors.
    • Electricity generation (2024): Ember Global Electricity Review. Shares of generation by fuel source.
    • Emissions trajectories (1990–2024): Annual national GHG totals from EDGAR v7.0 Minx et al., 2021 — the same dataset as the sectoral breakdown above, extended through 2024.
    • NDC targets: Nationally Determined Contributions compiled from the UNFCCC NDC Registry. These are countries' climate mitigation pledges under the Paris Agreement. Targets shown are current to COP26 (2021).
